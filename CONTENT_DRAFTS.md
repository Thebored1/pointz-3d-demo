## 2. Job-Site Moffett Unloading Guide ( /resources/job-site-unloading-guide )

  Page Title  : Job Site Preparation for Moffett Delivery | Point Zero Road Lines  
  H1  : The Ultimate Guide to Moffett Job Site Unloading  
  Subtitle  : Maximize efficiency and safety. How to prepare your construction site, facility, or residential project for seamless, self-unloading flatbed delivery.

  Introduction    
Truck-mounted Moffett forklifts revolutionize last-mile logistics. By eliminating the need for loading docks, customer-supplied forklifts, or expensive mobile site cranes, Point Zero Road Lines puts the power of complete delivery autonomy in the hands of our drivers. However, a smooth, 15-minute delivery requires proper site preparation. Follow this comprehensive guide to ensure your site is ready for Point Zero's heavy-duty, all-wheel-drive Moffett fleet.

### Step 1: Pre-Arrival Site Assessment & Ground Conditions
Our Moffett M55 and M8 units are equipped with all-wheel drive (AWD), differential locks, and rough-terrain pneumatic tires. They are built for construction sites, but they still obey the laws of physics.
      Ground Compaction  : The Moffett alone weighs approximately 6,000 lbs (2,720 kg). When fully loaded with 5,000 lbs of freight, the front axle bears extreme pressure. The staging area and transit path must be compacted soil, crushed gravel, asphalt, or concrete. Deep mud or loose sand will cause sinkage.
      Slope, Incline, and Grade  : Moffetts are incredibly stable, but for safe lifting, the unloading zone must not exceed a 5% grade (approx. 3 degrees). The transit path must not exceed a 15-degree lateral incline to prevent load shifting or rollover.
      Path Clearing  : The designated transit path from the parked truck to the final staging area must be cleared of framing debris, large rocks, deep ruts, and construction materials. 

### Step 2: Space, Maneuverability & Overhead Clearance
      Turning Radius & Width  : Moffetts feature a compact 3-wheel design with a zero-turn radius capability. However, accounting for the width of the machine and the payload (often 8 to 12 feet wide for lumber or drywall), we require a minimum access width of 10 feet to maneuver comfortably between structures, gates, or fencing.
      Vertical Overhead Clearance  : Identify and communicate any low-hanging hydro lines, telecommunication cables, tree branches, or scaffolding. The mast height of our Moffetts requires a minimum of 11.5 feet (138 inches) of vertical clearance during standard transit, and up to 15 feet when lifting over obstacles.
      Truck Staging Zone  : The 53ft flatbed transporting the Moffett requires a safe, legal, and level parking zone (street, cul-de-sac, or large commercial driveway). The truck needs approximately 75 feet of total linear space to safely unmount the forklift from the rear bumper.

### Step 3: Freight Placement & Exclusion Zones
The greatest advantage of a Moffett is precise point placement. We place it exactly where your trades need it, reducing double-handling.
      Exclusion Zones  : During unloading, a strict 20-foot perimeter must be maintained around the Moffett. No site workers, pedestrians, or other machinery may enter this zone.
      Targeted Placement  : Designate specific staging areas prior to arrival (e.g., "Place 4 lifts of drywall by the east entrance," or "Spot roofing materials as close to the mechanical hoist as possible").
      Weight Limits  : Ensure individual skids, bundles, or crates do not exceed our maximum lift capacity of 5,500 lbs.
