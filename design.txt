OUR STORY

FOURTEEN YEARS OF
SHOWING UP.

We grew the way freight companies should — one satisfied shipper at a time. No overpromising, no mystery surcharges, no trucks that leave the yard before the pre-trip's done.

2012

One truck, one lane

Founded in a rented Bolton bay hauling steel between Hamilton and the GTA.

2016

Moffett piggyback added

First truck-mounted forklifts put on the road so crews could unload anywhere on site.

2020

Mississauga cross-dock

18,000 sq ft facility opened, letting us stage, sort and reload same-day freight.

2026

26 trucks, one standard

Dedicated lanes across Ontario with the same dispatch team that started it all.

HOW WE OPERATE

FOUR RULES WE DON'T BEND.

They're not posters in the lunchroom. They're how loads get planned, how drivers get hired and how we answer the phone at 5am.

01
On schedule, not on average

Appointment windows are commitments. If a load is going to slip, you hear it from us first — not from your customer.

02
Safety before speed

Full pre-trips, secured loads, CVOR-clean records. Every driver is trained on Moffett operation and load securement.

03
A real person on dispatch

No ticket queues. Call the desk between 6am and 8pm and you get someone who knows exactly where your trailer is.

04
We know these roads

The 401 at 4pm, the Bolton back routes, tight downtown sites. Local knowledge is why our loads land on time.
